Tortuga is a video player and provides no content. The addons you install, and the servers they point to, get your requests directly. Tortuga's own server is not in that path.

With a Tortuga account, Tortuga's server keeps your sign-in identity, your devices, what you sync and your profiles' tracker sign-ins. It can read them, and stores what you sync and the tracker sign-ins encrypted at rest. There is no end-to-end encryption.

Watch together sends what you play, and your profile's name, to the others in the room through Open Watch Party's public relays, or another relay you choose.

No ads, and Tortuga runs no analytics of its own. Your data is not sold. Delete your account at any time in Settings, under Account.

When the app crashes, or a fault stops one part of it while the rest keeps running, it sends a report to the developer's own crash-report server: what went wrong, the app's version, and the device's model and system. A report never holds your account, your addon links or your device's name. Turn crash reports off in Settings, under This device.

The full policy is at usetortuga.app/privacy.
