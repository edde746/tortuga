Tortuga is a video player for addons the user installs. It provides no content of its own. This build has no in-app list of addons to install: an addon is added only by entering or opening its link.

SIGNING IN
Tortuga asks for an account at first launch, because a profile's setup (its addons, library and settings) is entered once and then follows the person to every device. Choose Sign in with Google and use the demo account in the Sign-In Information fields (demo@usetortuga.app).

SEEING SOMETHING PLAY
A new profile starts with a catalog addon (Cinemeta) and a subtitle addon (OpenSubtitles). They list titles but have no streams, so nothing plays until an addon with streams is installed. Our demo addon streams full-length public-domain films from the Internet Archive and Blender Foundation open movies (CC BY) from download.blender.org, straight from those sites:
1. Open Settings, then Addons, and choose Install addon.
2. Enter https://usetortuga.app/demo/manifest.json, choose Continue, then Install.
3. On Home, the last row is "Tortuga demo". Open Tears of Steel, choose Play, then pick the Tortuga demo stream: it plays from download.blender.org. Night of the Living Dead (1968), in the same row, plays from the Internet Archive.

DELETING THE ACCOUNT
Settings, then Account, then Delete account deletes the account and everything the server keeps for it, and signs out every device. Without the app, https://usetortuga.app/support/#delete says how to ask by email.
